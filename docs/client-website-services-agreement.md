Any change to scope, price, timing, or Deliverables must be approved by both parties in writing, including by email. Provider does not have to begin changed or additional work until the parties agree on its price and schedule.

## 2. Client responsibilities

Client will:

1. provide accurate content, brand materials, credentials, approvals, and feedback reasonably needed for the project;
2. identify one person authorized to give instructions and approvals for Client;
3. review submissions and respond within **[5] business days**; and
4. confirm that Client has the right to use all text, images, trademarks, data, and other materials it supplies.

Provider may reasonably extend deadlines when Client delays a required response, approval, payment, credential, or item. Provider is not responsible for errors in Client-supplied information or materials.

## 3. Fees, invoices, and payment

Client will pay the fees in the Project Schedule. Unless the Project Schedule says otherwise:

- the initial payment is due when this Agreement is signed and before work begins;
- remaining invoices are due within **[10] calendar days** after the invoice date;
- Client must submit any information its accounts-payable process requires no later than **[DATE OR “before work begins”]**;
- undisputed recurring fees are billed **[monthly/annually] in advance**; and
- Client will reimburse only expenses approved by Client in writing before they are incurred.

If Client disputes an invoice, Client must explain the specific dispute in writing within **[7] calendar days** after receipt and timely pay the undisputed amount. Provider may pause work or hosting after giving **[5] business days’** written notice of an overdue, undisputed payment. Project dates will move accordingly. Late charges, if any, will be the lesser of **[1% per month]** or the maximum amount allowed by law.

Taxes are Client’s responsibility except taxes on Provider’s net income.

## 4. Review, revisions, and acceptance

The project includes the revision rounds stated in the Project Schedule. A revision round is one consolidated set of Client comments delivered at one time. Additional rounds, new features, or a change in previously approved direction are additional work.

Client will accept a Deliverable or provide a specific written list of material ways it fails to match the Project Schedule within **[5] business days** after delivery. Provider will use reasonable efforts to correct confirmed nonconformities. A Deliverable is accepted when Client approves it in writing, uses it in production, or does not provide a timely list of material nonconformities.

## 5. Schedule and launch

Any delivery or launch date is an estimate unless the Project Schedule expressly labels it a guaranteed deadline. Provider is not responsible for delays caused by Client, a third-party provider, domain or DNS access, platform review, force majeure, or events outside Provider’s reasonable control.

Provider will not change nameservers or DNS records, publish to a production domain, activate analytics, or activate a form or third-party integration unless the Project Schedule expressly authorizes that work and Client provides the necessary access and approval. Client is responsible for preserving its email-related DNS records and maintaining access to its domain registrar.

## 6. Ownership and licenses

### 6.1 Client materials

Client retains ownership of materials it supplies. Client gives Provider a limited license to copy, modify, and use those materials only as reasonably necessary to perform the Services.

### 6.2 Final custom work

Upon Provider’s receipt of all amounts due for the project, Provider assigns to Client all transferable copyright rights Provider owns in the final, custom Deliverables created specifically for Client and identified in the Project Schedule. To the extent any such Deliverable qualifies as a “work made for hire,” the parties also agree that it is specially commissioned as a work made for hire. Drafts, rejected concepts, and unused work are not assigned.

### 6.3 Provider tools and third-party materials

Provider retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, know-how, methods, scripts, utilities, workflows, generic components, and improvements that are not uniquely created for Client (**“Provider Tools”**). To the extent Provider Tools are embedded in a paid Deliverable, Provider grants Client a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, copy, modify, and distribute them only as part of or in connection with that Deliverable.

Open-source software, fonts, stock media, platform components, and other third-party materials remain subject to their own licenses and terms. Provider will identify material third-party items when reasonably practicable. Client is responsible for any third-party fees the Project Schedule assigns to Client.

## 7. Hosting, maintenance, and third-party services

Hosting, maintenance, content updates, monitoring, backups, analytics, domain registration, email, accessibility monitoring, privacy compliance services, and third-party integrations are included only if listed in the Project Schedule.

Third-party services may change, fail, suspend access, or change their prices or terms. Provider does not control and is not responsible for a third party’s service, but will provide reasonable assistance within any support plan Client purchases. Provider may pass through an approved third-party price increase on written notice.

If Provider manages hosting, Client may request a current export of Client-owned site files after paying all undisputed amounts due. Provider will not hold Client-owned content or domain credentials solely to force payment of a genuinely disputed amount.

## 8. Legal and compliance responsibilities

Provider will use reasonable professional care in performing the Services. Unless expressly listed in the Project Schedule, Provider is not Client’s lawyer, privacy consultant, tax adviser, or accessibility auditor and does not promise that the website will satisfy every law, regulation, industry rule, or accessibility standard.

Client is responsible for its business claims, licenses, offers, pricing, terms, privacy disclosures, cookie choices, data collection practices, and legal compliance. Client will review and approve all factual and legal content before launch. Provider will not knowingly publish content that Client identifies as unlawful or infringing.

## 9. Confidentiality

Each party may receive non-public business, technical, or customer information from the other (**“Confidential Information”**). The receiving party will use it only for this project, protect it with reasonable care, and disclose it only to people who need it for the project and are bound to protect it. Confidential Information does not include information that is public through no breach, already lawfully known, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source. A party may disclose information when legally required after giving advance notice when legally permitted.

## 10. Warranties and disclaimer

Provider warrants for **[30] days** after launch that the final Deliverables will materially conform to the Project Schedule when used in the supported browsers and environment listed there. Client’s exclusive remedy under this warranty is correction of a reproducible nonconformity reported during that period.

Except for the express warranty above, the Services and Deliverables are provided **“as is”** to the maximum extent allowed by law. Provider does not guarantee search rankings, traffic, leads, sales, uninterrupted hosting, or any particular business result.

## 11. Indemnity

Client will defend and indemnify Provider from third-party claims, damages, and reasonable legal fees arising from Client-supplied materials, Client’s products or services, or Client’s instructions, except to the extent caused by Provider’s breach of this Agreement or willful misconduct. Provider will promptly notify Client of a covered claim and allow Client to control the defense, but Client may not settle a claim in a way that admits fault by or imposes a non-monetary obligation on Provider without Provider’s written consent.

## 12. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent allowed by law, neither party will be liable under this Agreement for lost profits, lost revenue, loss of goodwill, loss of data, or indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, or consequential damages.

Except for payment obligations, misuse of the other party’s intellectual property, breach of confidentiality, indemnity obligations, fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct, each party’s total liability arising from this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable to Provider under the applicable Project Schedule during the **[six] months** before the event giving rise to the claim.

## 13. Term and termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the Services are completed, unless ended earlier under this section. Either party may end it if the other party materially breaches and does not cure the breach within **[10] calendar days** after written notice.

Client may cancel the project for convenience on written notice. In that event, Client will pay for work performed through the cancellation date, accepted non-cancellable commitments, and any cancellation fee expressly stated in the Project Schedule. Provider will deliver completed, paid-for work in its then-current form.

Either party may end recurring hosting or support on **[30] days’** written notice, unless the Project Schedule states a different minimum term. At termination, Client must pay all undisputed amounts for Services performed through the termination date. Sections concerning payment, ownership, confidentiality, disclaimers, indemnity, liability, disputes, and other provisions that logically should survive will survive.

## 14. Independent contractor

Provider is an independent contractor and controls the manner and means of performing the Services. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, fiduciary, franchise, or agency relationship. Neither party may bind the other. Provider may serve other clients and may use qualified subcontractors while remaining responsible for their work and confidentiality obligations.

## 15. Disputes and governing law

The parties will first try in good faith to resolve a dispute through a meeting between people authorized to settle it. If the dispute is not resolved within **[15] days** after a written request to meet, either party may pursue available legal remedies.

The state and federal courts located in **[COUNTY, STATE]** will have exclusive jurisdiction, and each party consents to that venue. **[Optional: The prevailing party in an action to enforce this Agreement may recover reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs to the extent allowed by law.]**

This Agreement and its Project Schedule are the entire agreement about the Services and replace prior discussions and proposals about them. If they conflict, the Project Schedule controls only for the project-specific business term at issue. Any amendment must be in writing and accepted by both parties.
